    On the phone: the 200 × 40 header slot

    Upload: a horizontal logo, a square mark, optional light and dark versions. SVG best, PNG at least 400px wide. On upload: transparent edges are trimmed and the aspect ratio stored, so padding never shrinks the logo. On the phone: scaled to fit 200 × 40, never stretched or cropped, never upscaled, cached for offline use. QR stays plain: no logo inside the code. Shown on Work only; Personal and Links keep the Cardshare logo.

          The real v2 app

          Sign in on the phone with an email at harbour.example, nordlys.example or pinepost.example to see each organisation's card. Phones check in when the app opens and once a day. A work card that can't check in for 14 days is paused until it can. "Check in now" asks straight away, so you can watch an edit or a removal arrive.

          Microsoft

          Permissions requested

          Cardshare (unverified prototype) wants these permissions for everyone in your organisation:

          • Read all users' full profiles (User.Read.All, application)
          • Read organisational branding (OrganizationalBranding.Read.All, application)

          Read-only. Cardshare can't change anything in your directory. Mobile numbers are never requested.

          Accepting gives consent for your whole organisation. You can remove it at any time in Microsoft Entra admin centre → Enterprise applications.
