Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and no trial. If it helps you, you can buy me a beer.

Does the other person need anything?

No. Their phone camera reads the QR and offers to save you, on iPhone or Android.

Where are my details kept?

In your own browser, on your phone. There is no account and no server, so they go nowhere else.

Can I share more than my number?

Yes. Add your WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram, website and other links, then tap Links to show a QR for any one of them.

How do I keep it on my phone?

iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open the browser menu, then Install app. It opens like any other app, even with no signal.

What if I change phones?

Your cards live only on the phone you made them on, so you would type them in again on the new one. It takes about a minute.
